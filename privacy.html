Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-21 · Applies to version 1.2.1

TL;DR

Nota6 does not collect, transmit, or store any user data. It runs entirely on your Mac and reads only the Markdown files you explicitly open.

What we collect

Nothing. Nota6 has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers.

What we store locally

To preserve your settings between launches, Nota6 saves the following to your own Mac (in standard user defaults, never transmitted off-device):

  • Recently opened files and folders (paths only)
  • Per-file scroll positions
  • Theme, font size, color scheme, and reading width preferences
  • Outline panel visibility and immersive-mode state
  • Auto-refresh toggle state
  • "Don't ask again" flags for the welcome / default-app prompts
  • macOS security-scoped bookmarks for folders you grant access to (so they reopen across launches without re-prompting)

None of this data ever leaves your Mac. Uninstall removes everything; you can also reset preferences via defaults delete com.coldplay.nota6.

What we render

Nota6 renders the Markdown files you open. If those files reference remote images (e.g. https://... URLs), the rendering engine (Apple's WebKit) will fetch those images, just as Safari would. We do not proxy, intercept, or log these requests.

KaTeX math, Mermaid diagrams, and syntax-highlighted code are rendered locally with libraries bundled inside the app. No network requests are made for rendering.

Sandbox

Nota6 runs in the macOS App Sandbox with only read-only access to files you explicitly select. It cannot read your other documents, your camera, your microphone, your contacts, or any other resource on your Mac.

Children

Nota6 is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any data, from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and post a note in the app's release notes.
